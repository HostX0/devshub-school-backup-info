Your connection, explained

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

DevsHub School Backups uses an authorized school operator’s Google Drive account to store and verify encrypted recovery copies for one school. Google access is limited to files created by this application.

What the service accesses

The connection requests the drive.file permission. It creates and manages its backup files and folders, reads their metadata, downloads them for integrity verification, and removes older eligible copies under the retention policy. It also checks available Drive storage.

The service does not request access to all existing Drive files, Gmail, contacts or calendars. It does not use Google access to read unrelated personal documents.

What is stored and why

The dedicated backup folder contains encrypted archives needed to recover the school system, together with verification information such as timestamps, file sizes and checksums. The encrypted archives may contain the school records, uploaded documents and settings maintained in the school system.

These copies are used for recovery and integrity checking. They are not used for advertising, sold, or used for AI processing or model training.

How access is handled

Archives are encrypted before transfer. The operator keeps an independent recovery key separate from the backup destination. Google authorization credentials are held in protected server configuration and are not included in application logs or the public website.

Google processes the uploaded encrypted files as the storage provider under the operator’s Google account. Authorized school administrators and the account operator control recovery access. The service does not make the backup folder public or share it with advertising services.

Retention and storage limits

The default policy targets the latest copy for seven distinct days and four distinct weeks. Copies that satisfy both periods are counted once. The dedicated backup storage budget is capped at 1 GiB.

Older eligible copies are removed only after a new copy has been uploaded, downloaded and verified. If transfer or verification fails, existing copies are preserved. Incomplete or unrecognized files are left for the operator to handle. Storage limits or failures can therefore prevent the normal retention cycle from completing.

The connection does not purchase extra storage. The operator’s available Google storage is shared with other Google services.

Your controls

You can revoke this application’s access at any time from Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete copies already stored in Drive.

The authorized operator can delete retained copies in Google Drive and stop the backup connection. Deleting recovery copies may affect the school’s ability to restore its system. Copies kept separately by the school are managed by its administrator.

This information website

These pages explain the backup connection. They contain no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, sign-in forms or student records. The website hosting provider may process ordinary request information to deliver the pages.

Questions or changes

For privacy, authorization, deletion or recovery questions, contact the school administrator or the operator who configured this connection. If the service’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a revised date.
