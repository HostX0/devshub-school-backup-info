DevsHub School Backups helps the authorized operator of a single school keep an additional recovery copy outside the school system’s server.

A limited connection for school backups

The operator connects their own Google Drive account. The service uploads encrypted school backup archives to a dedicated folder and checks the downloaded copies before reporting a successful transfer. It is an administrative backup tool, not a student or parent sign-in service.

01

Encrypt before transfer

School recovery archives are encrypted before they leave the server. The operator manages the recovery key separately from the backup destination.

02

Limit Drive access

The connection uses Google’s limited file permission. It manages files created by this application, without requesting access to all existing Drive files.

03

Keep useful recovery points

The default policy targets seven daily and four weekly copies, with overlapping copies counted once, within a maximum 1 GiB backup budget.

  • Existing recovery copies are retained when a new copy cannot be verified. Older eligible copies are removed only after a new copy passes verification.
  • The connection does not purchase Google storage. Available space is shared with the operator’s other Google services.
  • The operator can revoke Google account access at any time and manage retained copies in their own Drive.

For access, recovery or privacy questions, contact the school administrator or the operator who configured this backup connection. Read the privacy policy for details.
